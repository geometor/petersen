Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (1866 / 1879)¶

Methoder og Theorier til Løsning af geometriske Konstruktionsopgaver (Kjøbenhavn: Karl Schønbergs Forlag, 1866; 2nd rev. ed., Andr. Fred. Høst & Søns Forlag, 1879) · Authorized English translation by Sophus Haagensen, Cand. Polyt. C. E. (Copenhagen: Andr. Fred. Høst & Son / London: Sampson Low, Marston, Searle & Rivington, 1879).
