.. _bibliography:

Bibliography
============

.. rubric:: Primary Editions, Classical Sources & Heuristic Descendants

.. _cite-hilbert-1899:

**David Hilbert** — *Grundlagen der Geometrie*
   B. G. Teubner, Leipzig, 1899.

   Axiomatic foundation of geometry and Chapter VII gauge-and-straightedge constructibility criteria (GEOMETOR hilbert).

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

**Euclid (trans. Sir Thomas L. Heath)** — *The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements*
   Cambridge University Press, c. 300 BCE / 1908.

   Canonical axiomatic foundation of compass-and-straightedge geometry (GEOMETOR euclid & elements).

.. _cite-klein-1895:

**Felix Klein** — *Vorträge über ausgewählte Fragen der Elementargeometrie*
   B. G. Teubner, Leipzig (English trans. Famous Problems of Elementary Geometry, 1897), 1895.

   Classic synthesis of straightedge-and-compass, compass-only, and transcendental constructibility.

.. _cite-lindemann-1882:

**Ferdinand von Lindemann** — *Über die Zahl pi*
   Mathematische Annalen 20, 213–225, 1882.

   Proved the transcendence of pi three years after Petersen's remark on p. 101 of the 1879 English edition.

.. _cite-polya-1962:

**George Pólya** — *Mathematical Discovery: On Understanding, Learning, and Teaching Problem Solving (Vol. I)*
   John Wiley & Sons, New York, 1962.

   Chapters 1 and 2 build heuristic pedagogy directly around Petersen's Pattern of Two Loci and Pattern of Similar Figures.

.. _cite-schroeter-1874:

**Heinrich Schröter** — *Die Steinersche Auflösung der Malfattischen Aufgabe*
   Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 77, 230–244, 1874.

   First proof of Steiner's Malfatti construction via circle inversions, simplified by Petersen in 1879 (pp. 96–97).

.. _cite-steiner-1826:

**Jakob Steiner** — *Einige geometrische Betrachtungen*
   Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 1, 161–184, 1826.

   Introduces power of a point, radical axes, centres of similitude, and Steiner's construction of Malfatti's circles.

.. _cite-petersen-1866:

**Julius Petersen** — *Methoder og Theorier til Løsning af geometriske Konstruktionsopgaver, anvendte paa ca. 400 Opgaver*
   Karl Schønbergs Forlag, Kjøbenhavn, 1866.

   First Danish edition of Petersen's treatise on geometric construction heuristics.

.. _cite-petersen-1871:

**Julius Petersen** — *Om Ligninger, der løses ved Kvadratrod*
   Kjøbenhavn (Doctoral Dissertation, University of Copenhagen), 1871.

   Petersen's doctoral thesis on algebraic equations solvable by square roots, cited in the Appendix (p. 98).

.. _cite-petersen-1878:

**Julius Petersen** — *Theorie der algebraischen Gleichungen*
   Andr. Fred. Høst & Sohn, Kopenhagen, 1878.

   German edition of Petersen's treatise on Galois theory and quadratic constructibility, cited on p. 98.

.. _cite-petersen-1891:

**Julius Petersen** — *Die Theorie der regulären graphs*
   Acta Mathematica 15, 193–220, 1891.

   Foundational paper of modern graph theory.

.. _cite-petersen-1879:

**Julius Petersen (trans. Sophus Haagensen)** — *Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions, Applied to 410 Problems*
   Andr. Fred. Høst & Son, Copenhagen / Sampson Low, Marston, Searle & Rivington, London, 1879.

   Authorized English edition from the expanded 2nd Danish edition (102 pages, 410 problems).

.. _cite-altshiller-court-1952:

**Nathan Altshiller-Court** — *College Geometry: An Introduction to the Modern Geometry of the Triangle and the Circle (2nd ed.)*
   Barnes & Noble, New York, 1952.

   Standard modern synthetic geometry treatise directly extending Petersen's Chapter I–III methods (GEOMETOR altshiller-court).

.. _cite-pappus-synagoge:

**Pappus of Alexandria** — *Synagoge (Mathematical Collection), Book VII — Domain of Analysis*
   ed. F. Hultsch (1877) / A. Jones (1986), c. 340 CE.

   Classical formulation of geometric analysis and synthesis, harmonic ranges, and Apollonius's lost Tangencies and Plane Loci (GEOMETOR pappus).

.. _cite-wantzel-1837:

**Pierre Laurent Wantzel** — *Recherches sur les moyens de reconnaître si un problème de Géométrie peut se résoudre avec la règle et le compas*
   Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 2, 366–372, 1837.

   First rigorous proof of the impossibility of trisecting the general angle and duplicating the cube.

.. _cite-lemoine-1902:

**Émile Lemoine** — *Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques*
   C. Naud, Paris (Scientia, no. 18), 1902.

   Operational complexity calculus for Euclidean constructions (GEOMETOR lemoine).
