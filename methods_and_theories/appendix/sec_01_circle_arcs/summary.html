In This Summary

  • Architectural Overview (pp. 95–97)

Architectural Overview (pp. 95–97)¶

Appendix Section 1 investigates the intersection of arcs of circles (Ueber den Durchschnitt von Kreisbogen, pp. 95–97):

  • Rolling a tangent line around a curvilinear polygon formed by circular arcs proves (Theorem 1, p. 89) that the sum of the arc-measures of the sides (signed positively or negatively) plus the sum of the exterior supplement angles equals \(4R\) (\(360^\circ\)), streamlining angle-chasing across intricate circle configurations.
