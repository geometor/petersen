Architectural Overview (pp. 5–21)¶

Chapter I, Part A builds the complete dictionary of elementary point loci (a–k) so that whenever a problem reduces to locating a single unknown point \(P\) subject to two conditions \(\mathcal{C}_1 \wedge \mathcal{C}_2\), omitting \(\mathcal{C}_2\) and \(\mathcal{C}_1\) in turn produces two straight-line or circular loci \(\mathcal{L}_1, \mathcal{L}_2\) whose intersections \(\mathcal{L}_1 \cap \mathcal{L}_2\) solve the problem directly.

Complete Dictionary of Point Loci (a – k)¶

Petersen’s Eleven Fundamental Point Loci (pp. 7–14)¶

Locus

Detached Condition on Point \(P\)

Resulting Constructible Curve

a

Given distance \(r\) from fixed point \(O\) (or tangent length / visual angle to a circle)

Circle \((O, r)\) (or concentric circles \(O, R \pm r\))

b

Given distance \(h\) from fixed straight line \(l\) (or fixed triangle area on base \(AB\))

Pair of straight lines parallel to \(l\) at distance \(\pm h\)

c

Equal distances \(PA = PB\) from two fixed points

Perpendicular bisector of segment \(AB\)

d

Equal perpendicular distances from two fixed lines \(l_1, l_2\)

Pair of orthogonal angle bisectors of \((l_1, l_2)\)

e

Segment \(AB\) seen under given angle \(v\) (Pothenot’s Problem 19)

Pair of symmetric circular arcs on chord \(AB\) (single circle on diameter \(AB\) when \(v = 90^\circ\))

f

Given distance ratio \(PA : PB = m : n\) (\(m \neq n\))

Circle of Apollonius on harmonic diameter \(CC_1\) where \((A, B; C, C_1) = -1\)

g

Given ratio \(m : n\) of distances from two intersecting lines

Pair of lines through \(l_1 \cap l_2\) forming harmonical rays

h

Constant difference of squares \(PB^2 - PA^2 = a^2\) (or equal tangent powers to two circles)

Straight line \(\perp AB\) (radical axis, concurring at radical centre in Problem 30)

i

Constant sum of squares \(PA^2 + PB^2 = a^2\)

Circle centered at midpoint \(M\) of \(AB\) with \(r^2 = \tfrac{1}{2}a^2 - \tfrac{1}{4}AB^2\)

k

Constant sum or difference \(a\) of distances from two intersecting lines

Four segments of a rectangle (sum) plus eight infinite prolongations (difference)
