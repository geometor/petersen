Unit III: Chapter I, Part B — Loci of Lines & Tangential Envelopes — Summary¶

Structural & Heuristic Overview — Kapitel I.B — Geometriske Steder for Linier (l–p) (pp. 40–45 · Problems 220–245)

In This Summary

  • Architectural Overview (pp. 40–45)

  • Complete Dictionary of Line Loci (l – p)

Architectural Overview (pp. 40–45)¶

In Chapter I, Part B, Petersen dualizes the Method of Two Loci from unknown points to unknown straight lines. Omitting one condition on a sought line produces a one-parameter family of lines (locus of lines) that envelopes a constructible circle or passes through a fixed point (finite or at infinity). Intersecting two such line-loci reduces every two-condition secant or chord problem to drawing:

  1. The straight line joining two points (two point-pencils);

  2. The tangents from a point to a circle (one point-pencil + one circular envelope, Problem 16 / 220); or

  3. The four common tangents to two circles (two circular envelopes, Problem 137 / 221 / 275).

Complete Dictionary of Line Loci (l – p)¶

Petersen’s Five Fundamental Line Loci (pp. 40–41)¶

Locus

Detached Condition on Straight Line \(L\)

Resulting Envelope / Pencil Vertex

l

Cuts off chords of given length \(2c\) from given circle \((O, R)\)

Concentric envelope circle \((O, \sqrt{R^2 - c^2})\) (Problem 220)

m

Distances from two fixed points \(A, B\) have given ratio \(m : n\)

Two fixed points dividing \(AB\) internally and externally in ratio \(m : n\)

n

Algebraic distances from \(A, B\) have given sum \(s\) (or given difference \(d\))

Circle centered at midpoint of \(AB\) with radius \(s/2\) (or two parallel directions at \(\infty\))

o

Divides inscribed angles on arc \(AB\) in a fixed ratio/difference (e.g. bisectors)

Fixed point on the opposite circular arc \(AB\) (Problems 232, 236)

p

Common chords of all circles through \(A, B\) meeting a fixed circle \(\Gamma\)

Fixed radical-centre point \(O \in AB\) (Problem 238, PPC Apollonius case)
