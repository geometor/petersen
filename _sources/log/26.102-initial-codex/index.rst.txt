Initial 3-Track Digital Scriptorium Codex & Exact Symbolic Pattern Library

Established ``geometor/petersen`` (``v0.1.0``) for **Julius Petersen's** *Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions, Applied to 410 Problems* (Copenhagen, 1866; Authorized English Edition translated by **Sophus Haagensen**, Cand. Polyt. C. E., Copenhagen & London, 1879).

Highlights
----------

* **Multimodal Archival Verification**: Inspected high-resolution page scans of the 1879 Høst & Son / Sampson Low edition (``pp. 1–102``) directly via multimodal vision—preserving Petersen's four-stage schema (*Analysis, Mode of Proceeding, Proof, Discussion*), point loci ``a``–``k``, tangential line loci ``l``–``p``, parallel translation, replacing, axial reflection, Chapter III *Theory of Revolution* :math:`(O, v, f)`, homothety, circle inversion, Monge/Gergonne/Malfatti circle systems, and quadratic radical-tower constructibility (including Petersen's 1879 pre-Lindemann :math:`\pi` remark on p. 101).
* **Synchronized 3-Track Book Codex**: Compiled seven ``:layout: book`` thematic units (``English 1879 · Summary · Notes``) and four apparatus registries (``concordance.rst``, ``bibliography.rst``, ``persons.rst``, ``glossary.rst``) with zero embedded ``py_file`` scripts.
* **Exact Symbolic ``geometor.model`` Constructions**: Implemented and proved eight 100% float-free SymPy propositions over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` and :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})`, paired with calibrated theme-adaptive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVGs and five Rich CLI demonstrations in ``demos/``.
