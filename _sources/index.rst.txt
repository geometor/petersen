GEOMETOR • petersen
===================

.. rubric:: Heuristic Methods & Exact Constructive Geometry of Julius Petersen (1839–1910)

Digital Scriptorium Book Codex, calibrated interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG figures, exact SymPy ``geometor.model`` pattern library, and cross-repository concordance for **Julius Petersen's** *Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions, Applied to 410 Problems* (Copenhagen, 1866; Authorized English Edition translated by **Sophus Haagensen**, 1879 — 100% Public Domain).

Book Codex
----------

*Methoder og Theorier til Løsning af geometriske Konstruktionsopgaver* (Kjøbenhavn: Karl Schønbergs Forlag, 1866; 2nd rev. ed., Andr. Fred. Høst & Søns Forlag, 1879) · Authorized English translation by **Sophus Haagensen**, Cand. Polyt. C. E. (Copenhagen: Andr. Fred. Høst & Son / London: Sampson Low, Marston, Searle & Rivington, 1879).

Petersen's landmark treatise systematizing how geometric constructions are *discovered*—transforming Euclidean theorems into general problem-solving heuristics across **Loci of Points**, **Loci of Lines (Envelopes)**, **Multiplication of Curves & The Method of Similitude**, **Inverse Figures**, **Parallel Translation**, **Replacing & Axial Reflection**, **Spiral Revolution Around a Pivot Point**, **Apollonius/Malfatti Circle Systems**, and **Algebraic Straightedge-and-Compass Constructibility**.
