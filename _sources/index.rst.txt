===============
Julius Petersen
===============

.. image:: _static/medallion.svg
   :alt: Julius Petersen Medallion
   :class: repo-medallion-hero

**Julius Petersen** (1839–1910) turned Euclidean construction from an art of lucky guesswork into a disciplined heuristic science. In *Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions* (1866; English ed. 1879), Petersen organized **410 classical problems** around systematic strategies—intersecting geometric loci, parallel translation, axial reflection, homothety, spiral rotation, and circle inversion. This digital archive presents his three-track Book Codex alongside calibrated vector figures, folio provenance, and exact SymPy transformation models.
