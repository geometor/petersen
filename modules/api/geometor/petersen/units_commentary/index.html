geometor.petersen.units_commentary¶

Structured Summary and Notes companion tracks for geometor-petersen.

Provides canonical and unit specifications, summaries, and notes for the 3-track synchronized Book Codex (English 1879 · Summary · Notes) of Julius Petersen’s Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (1879).
